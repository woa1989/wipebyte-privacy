WipeByte 隐私政策

生效日期与最后更新：2026 年 10 月 29 日

适用产品：WipeByte for macOS（Mac App Store 版与官网直接下载版）

1. 概述

WipeByte 是一款 macOS 磁盘清理与空间管理工具，由 343 科技公司（Nanyang 343 Technology Co., Ltd.）开发。我们尽量不收集任何个人数据：App Store 版本除了 Apple 提供的内购与商店更新服务外，不连接任何由我们自建的服务器，也不会把你的文件、使用记录或设备信息发送给我们。

2. 我们不收集的信息

  • 不收集账号信息。WipeByte 不需要注册或登录，没有账号体系。
  • 不收集文件内容。扫描、分析、清理、卸载与碎纸等操作全部在你的 Mac 本地完成。文件名、路径、文件内容都不会上传到我们的服务器。
  • 不收集使用统计。App 内没有分析 SDK、广告 SDK、崩溃上报 SDK 或任何第三方追踪代码。
  • 不收集支付信息。内购由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理；我们不接触你的姓名、支付方式、账单地址或 Apple ID 凭据。

3. 文件访问与权限

为了统计磁盘空间并在你确认后执行清理，WipeByte 会在你授权后访问相应的磁盘或文件夹（例如系统「完全磁盘访问」权限，或在系统文件夹选择器中手动选择的目录）。这些访问仅用于在本机生成扫描结果、预览待删除项目，以及执行你明确确认的删除操作。

App Store 版本在沙盒内运行：清理与卸载会把项目移到废纸篓，清空废纸篓后才真正释放空间；App 不会绕过系统权限，也不会把你未授权的位置列入清理范围。

4. App 内购买

订阅（按年 / 按月）与永久版购买通过 Apple 的 StoreKit 完成，交易与收据由 Apple 管理。订阅的续订、取消与退款均遵循 Apple 的规则；你可以在 macOS 的「App Store 账户设置」中随时管理或取消订阅。恢复购买同样由 Apple 处理。

5. 官网直接下载版（CDN）的额外说明

如果你使用的是从官网直接下载的 WipeByte 版本，该版本使用激活码服务：在领取试用、激活授权或检查更新时，App 会向我们自建的授权服务器发送由本机硬件标识生成的不可逆哈希值（不包含原始硬件序列号）以及你输入的激活码、版本号等必要信息。这些信息仅用于授权校验、试用资格判断与更新分发，不包含任何文件内容或使用记录。Mac App Store 版本不会连接该授权服务器。

6. 数据共享与出售

我们不出售、不出租、不以营销为目的共享任何用户数据。仅在以下必要情形涉及第三方：

  • Apple：处理 App Store 购买、收据验证与商店更新，适用 Apple 的隐私政策。
  • GitHub Pages：托管本隐私政策页面，适用 GitHub 的隐私声明。
  • 自建授权服务器：仅官网直接下载版使用，处理上述假名化设备标识与激活码。

7. 数据保留与删除

Mac App Store 版本不会在我们的服务器上存储你的数据。官网直接下载版的授权记录只在提供授权服务所需的期间内保留；如需删除相关记录，可通过下方联系方式与我们联系。

8. 儿童隐私

WipeByte 不面向儿童，也不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。由于 App Store 版本不收集个人数据，正常使用不会产生此类信息；如你认为有相关信息被误收集，请联系我们。

9. 政策变更

如果本政策发生变更，我们会在本页面更新「最后更新」日期。涉及数据处理的重大变更，我们会通过 App 更新说明或本页面显著位置告知。

WipeByte Privacy Policy

Effective date and last updated: October 29, 2026

Applies to WipeByte for macOS (Mac App Store version and direct-download version)

1. Overview

WipeByte is a macOS disk cleanup and storage management app developed by Nanyang 343 Technology Co., Ltd. (343 Technology). We deliberately collect as little personal data as possible. Apart from Apple’s in-app purchase and App Store update services, the Mac App Store version does not contact any server operated by us, and it does not send your files, usage history, or device information to us.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No account data. WipeByte requires no registration or sign-in and has no account system.
  • No file contents. Scanning, analysis, cleaning, uninstalling, and shredding all happen locally on your Mac. File names, paths, and contents are never uploaded to our servers.
  • No usage analytics. The app contains no analytics SDK, advertising SDK, crash-reporting SDK, or third-party tracking code.
  • No payment data. In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, payment method, billing address, or Apple ID credentials.

3. File Access and Permissions

To measure disk usage and to clean only after you confirm, WipeByte accesses disks or folders you authorize (for example, by granting Full Disk Access or by choosing a folder in the system’s folder picker). That access is used solely to produce on-device scan results, preview items to be removed, and perform deletions you explicitly confirm.

The Mac App Store version runs in the App Sandbox: cleaning and uninstalling move items to the Trash, and space is reclaimed only after you empty the Trash. The app never bypasses system permissions and never includes locations you have not authorized.

4. In-App Purchases

Subscriptions (yearly / monthly) and the lifetime purchase are handled through Apple’s StoreKit, and transactions and receipts are managed by Apple. Renewal, cancellation, and refunds follow Apple’s rules. You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your macOS App Store account settings. Restoring purchases is also handled by Apple.

5. Note for the Direct-Download (CDN) Version

If you use the direct-download version from our website, it relies on an activation-code service. When claiming a trial, activating a license, or checking for updates, the app sends our activation server a one-way hash derived from your Mac’s hardware identifier (the raw hardware serial/UUID is never sent), together with the activation code or version information you provide. This data is used only for license validation, trial eligibility, and update delivery, and it never includes file contents or usage history. The Mac App Store version does not contact this activation server.

6. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell, rent, or share user data for marketing purposes. Third parties are involved only where necessary:

  • Apple — for App Store purchases, receipt validation, and store updates, subject to Apple’s privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this privacy policy page, subject to GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Our activation server — used only by the direct-download version, to process the pseudonymous device identifier and activation code described above.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

The Mac App Store version stores no data about you on our servers. Activation records for the direct-download version are retained only as long as needed to provide the licensing service. To request deletion of such records, contact us using the details below.

8. Children’s Privacy

WipeByte is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the Mac App Store version collects no personal data, such information is not generated in normal use. If you believe any such information has been collected in error, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the “last updated” date on this page. For material changes involving data handling, we will also announce them in the app’s release notes or prominently on this page.
